About Me.

Before Lockheed Martin Space, I graduated from Carnegie Mellon University with a Bacholor in Computer Science and a minor in Software Engineering. I have interned at multiple companies in the past, including Lockheed Martin, MIDL Technology, and more.

I strive to be involved in my community. Previously, I held three positions on the executive board of Carnegie Mellon’s Habitat for Humanity, including one that involved building homes for those in need in Houston, Texas. Other community involvement activities I have done include studying abroad in Spain, participating in Students Using Data for Social Good, and more.

I am currently conducting research with Professor Adam Perer at Carnegie Mellon University on bias and discrimination in generative AI models. Our work focuses on revealing patterns of racial, gender, and cultural bias through visualization and clustering techniques. Through this work, we aim to push for greater accountability and empathy in the development of emerging technologies.

I strive in all sorts of technical and creative environments. I love Python, design, theoretical computer science, algorithm design, research, machine learning / artifical intelligence, model design and optimization, and more.

Going forward, I always look forward to new opportunities. If you would like to contact me, please feel free to reach me at elislothower@gmail.com.
